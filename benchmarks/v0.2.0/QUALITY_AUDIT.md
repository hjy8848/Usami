# v0.2 task quality audit

Status: executable automated quality certificates; **AWAITING EXTERNAL HUMAN REVIEW**. No human ratings have been supplied. See machine-generated `coverage.json`, `review-examples.json`, `verification.json` and, when available, `pilot-report.json` for measured facts.

The public suite uses workflow-conditioned valid initial states. It is a curated candidate grid, not a census of all possible worlds or a claim that every seed is an independent structure. Exact family counts and independent structure counts are reported separately. A family label is not part of the structural hash. Failed and unpaid restoration are reported as branches of the same business family.

Mandatory automated gates regenerate the world, reconstruct the independent customer goal and capability domain, search legal routes, execute witnesses in the actual Engine, evaluate the independent Oracle, verify frame preservation, replay every event, and recompute canonical signatures. Refusal certificates prove specific permission, owner or financial constraints, not global impossibility inferred from failed search. Incompatible equality goals are rejected; further compatibility is established through bounded legal reachability. Failed search candidates cannot enter the suite.

Only minimal witnesses under single-transition omission are retained. Counterfactual omission edges can include mediated effects; edge counts do not mean independently direct dependencies. Prerequisite operation types are called necessary only when exclusion search finishes without a route **inside the frozen finite capability domain and search bounds**. No global unbounded completeness claim is made. See each task's `search_bounds`, `search_complete`, `necessary_operation_types` and actual Engine/Oracle/replay evidence.

The Service sees a natural business objective, authenticated identity, policy configuration and tool schemas. It sees neither witnesses nor dependency graphs. Exact allowed writes exist before model execution and are distinct from business permission. Alternatives include payment methods and independent legal action orders. Not every task has multiple alternatives, and longer sequences are not automatically harder. Slot and plan changes cannot alter unrelated tenants, the designated owner, user profiles or unrelated ledger entries.

Review package: `review-examples.json` samples every workflow family in every public split and every observed dependency depth. Each example includes the customer request, relevant initial state, identity/roles, joint terminal predicates, allowed frame, witness/policy certificates, alternatives and scoring explanation. This is evaluator-only material and must never be sent as a Service prompt.

External reviewers should independently rate (1–5) and explain:

1. Business realism: would a SaaS administrator plausibly make this request?
2. Clarity: is the goal objectively defined, with necessary preferences and no accidental ambiguity?
3. Non-leakage: does wording avoid revealing a reference sequence or hidden answer?
4. Authorization: does the fixed customer legitimately express the scope without identity switching?
5. Reasoning: are dependencies necessary, and are independent branches clearly distinguished?
6. Ground truth: do rules, terminal goals, accounting and preservation match the stated request?
7. Scoring independence: does replay/state evidence support the outcome without relying on phrasing?

Record reviewer identity, date, rubric scores, objections and adjudication separately. Automated passes must not be represented as human approval.

Implemented limits: administrator provisioning of existing directory users; one fixed actor per session; simulated full payments, failed attempts, partial refunds and idempotency; one subscription per tenant, 30-day periods, no real payment processing. Seat changes affect configured capacity; this release does not model mid-cycle proration, tax, discounts, real email invitation acceptance, credit wallets, variable cycle lengths or multi-party sessions. Refunds correct accounting but do not implicitly terminate a service contract. These limits are explicit policy choices, not hidden scoring exceptions.

Validation is in-distribution on new worlds, with payment/refusal/recovery diagnostics. Test Demo holds out membership–subscription–billing goal combinations while their atomic skills are in Train. Demo is public and is not a blind test. Private generation writes only to ignored `private/` paths; this release does not pretend its public seeds are secret.

No equivalence or superiority to τ-bench / τ²-bench is claimed. Pilot runs establish empirical utility on this environment only; offline reference-proof runs establish software correctness. Model errors and incomplete coverage remain visible and never become official aggregate scores.

Pilot-driven scoring correction: JSON formatting is a diagnostic, not the business refusal criterion. An externally verified policy certificate, preserved world and its exact public code in the completed reply certify operational policy handling. Neither a write probe nor JSON formatting is required. This avoids language/format false negatives, while making no subjective claim about explanation rhetoric or truthfulness. No-op “completed” replies cannot certify refusal without either evidence path.
