# FulfillmentOps quality audit

Human business-realism review: **AWAITING HUMAN REVIEW**.

The frozen protocol contains 338 independently executable Service instances:
265 Train, 27 Validation and 46 Public Test Demo. These are 67 base business
goal/decision cases plus 271 distinct, verified in-progress workflow states.
All use one world seed. No task count comes from renamed IDs, display labels,
random seeds or an amount-only scaling sweep.

The conservative diversity figures use **base goal cases only**: 31 authored
business workflow families, 49 anonymous structures and 65 semantic decision
signatures. Additional remaining-work structures of progress instances are
reported separately in `coverage.json` and do not inflate those headline figures.
Several refusal branches belong to the same business family; separate rejection
codes are not counted as separate families. Family labels await independent review.

173 instances have verified paths containing at least three causally linked
state transitions (first-failure omission depth at least two). Maximum measured
depth is ten. There are 162 instances with multiple independently certified,
irreducible legal routes. Alternative stock lots and shipping-label preparation
before/after physical handling are actual business choices. Queries are excluded
from search paths and causal measurements. There are 665 actual execution
witnesses and 26 independent correct-refusal certificates.

An omission experiment removes one operation from a valid prefix and attempts
remaining operations under independent rules. The first invalid later operation
is a direct omission edge. Failures after an intervening failed operation are
reported separately as mediated evidence. These bounded witnesses do not claim
to prove necessity against every possible unlisted operation or arbitrary plan.
Search depth/node caps, exhaustion and all certified paths are frozen per task.

Train and Test Demo have zero structural overlap. Test retains Train-seen
operation abilities while holding out genuine joint workflow compositions.
Progress variants whose remaining work reduces to a Train-seen structure are
assigned to Train/Validation, rather than presented as a structural holdout.
The public Test Demo is not a private blind benchmark.

Initial state is legal, materialized with independently checked transitions.
Predicate/frame field allowlists protect resource identity and unrelated state.
Existing entity bindings cannot be exempted wholesale by a frame wildcard.
Whole-entity exemptions are restricted to new entities and permitted handling
record deletions. All certificates are re-executed with actual Engine, independent
Oracle and exact event replay; generator evidence is never Service input.

`TASK_EXAMPLES.md` is the complete layered appendix. `REVIEW_SAMPLE.md` and
`review-sample-55.json` provide a deterministic 55-case human review sample,
prioritizing family/split, identity, depth and refusal layers. No human rating has
been fabricated. Regenerate the sample with:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. python examples/fulfillment/review_sample.py benchmarks/fulfillment-v0.1
```

Open Discovery uses an unconditioned world and no task/recipe/family/witness.
Customer native intention tools distinguish questions from exact write intents.
A complete action batch validates before any authority is issued. Format repair
only re-emits fully specified original intentions, costs an actual Customer call,
and preserves both original and retry output/error records. It does not infer
authority from natural-language context or Service-generated arguments.

Known measurement limits: finite certified routes (up to four), synthetic external
simulation and authored rather than human-validated workflow families. Pilot
results measure selected actual attempts, not full benchmark model capability.
