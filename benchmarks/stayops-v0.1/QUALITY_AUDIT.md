# StayOps v0.1 quality audit

AWAITING HUMAN REVIEW. No external reviewer has rated realism and no τ-bench equivalence is claimed.

## Counting and split policy

The release has 302 actual certified initial-state instances from 83 base business goal/decision cases and 219 distinct proper workflow progress states, all at seed 51001. Progress comes from actual Reference-approved Engine execution and recertification, with existing records' immutable bindings protected. It does not add business families, base structure or base semantic counts. Duplicate initial-state/identity/goal/capability cases are removed. No ID/name/seed/date/amount-only replicas are added.

The 37-family taxonomy groups refusal branches with their related workflow, rather than treating each policy error as a new family. Headline diversity is 64 anonymous base structures and 82 base semantic decision signatures. Semantics includes actual policy, scoped role, payment, time, inventory and independent identity branches. Numeric parameters and dates are normalized; entity IDs are anonymous relationship references. Signature counts depend on the published definition and are not a theoretical lower bound on all possible workflows.

Train/Validation/Public Test Demo are 234/1/67. Test keeps unseen combinations of Train abilities. Train–Test structure intersection is empty; no Test operation or normalized terminal atomic ability is absent from Train. A target such as current_stay_id=stay_b1 uses the same relationship-reference ability as stay_b0, not an ID-dependent new skill. The small Validation split is a material evaluation limitation: it arose from conservative structure/semantic grouping with a single repeated stratum. It should be expanded in a later version before hyperparameter selection; this pilot is not a validation-quality estimate.

## Causal and route evidence

Atomic predicates compose into JointGoal, with field compatibility checks, explicit protected frames, bounded legal reachability search, independent actual transition validation and omission certificates. The release has 569 actual witnesses, 29 certified refusals, 112 tasks with at least three directly linked transitions, 124 tasks with multiple unique legal operation sequences and maximum direct omission-edge depth 10. Queries and no-op padding are excluded. Multiple routes require distinct operation sequences; duplicate equal capabilities do not inflate the route count.

Direct edges mean removing one operation causes a later operation to be the first policy failure. Subsequent failures following an already failed step are reported separately as mediated effects. Sequence length is not causal depth. These are certificates within the bounded capability/search domain, not exhaustive necessity over every possible hotel tool plan. The cap is four witnesses and 18 steps/8192 search nodes. Global task completion scores terminal goal, preserved frame and independent Oracle, not witness sequence equality.

## Business/rule evidence and review package

Coverage contains actual operation counts, omission rule-code edges, refusal totals and causal-depth distribution. Tests independently exercise overlapping vs adjacent dates, middle-night capacity, explicit expiry, scoped roles, guest/staff separation, idempotency/failure, deposit/full-balance/identity gates, maintenance/calendar boundaries, repricing, cancellation fees, refunds and atomic swaps. Refusal certificates check the stated rule with an unchanged complete frame; these do not mean every combination of every policy has been exhaustively tested.

TASK_EXAMPLES.md and review-examples.json contain exactly 55 stratified cases with full initial state, human-readable request, terminal goal/frame, explicit refusal evidence or certified routes, and incorrect-result criteria. Sampling covers split/family first, then identity/depth/kind and fills remaining samples. Human realism, policy appropriateness, family taxonomy and transferability remain awaiting review.

## Discovery integrity

Discovery freezes a plain generated world, not a task-conditioned state. Customer sees only scoped public/business observations and actual Service replies. It never receives tasks, family labels, witness, goal, private simulator fixture or hidden Oracle verdict. The role-native function schema requires exact intentions independent of Service; all actions validate before a grant. Writes have single-use grants and errors cost real tool/model budgets. Reference rejected attempts, successful Sandbox business violations, authorization-only anomalies and unscored failures remain separate.

Original malformed model actions and any bounded recovery are retained. Recovery must preserve exact action/message/order/parameters and cannot infer missing intent. Future Customer methods can use native actions or ask_service, while assert_comparable fixes the victim, identity, world, tools, source, budgets and trials. No Evolver, training, automatic Service repair or fixed Customer attack script is implemented.
