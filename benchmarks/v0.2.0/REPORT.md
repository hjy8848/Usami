# Usami SaaS Benchmark v0.2 release report

This release is an executable, curated single-domain SaaS benchmark. It implements 14 business writes and four core queries, with an authenticated context projection, independent Reference policy, actual Engine transitions, Oracle evidence and deterministic replay. No claim of equivalence or superiority to τ-bench / τ²-bench is made.

| Train | 170 | 23 | Atomic skills and representative compositions |
| Validation | 68 | 23 | In-distribution new worlds, with refusal/recovery diagnostics |

| Total | 278 | 28 | 236 achievable, 42 policy-constrained refusal |

There are 22 workflow labels and **21 business families**; failed and unpaid billing restoration share one business family. They are not counted as two independent structures simply because their labels differ. The suite has **47 semantic decision signatures**, effective structural diversity **26.54**, and 80 tasks with more than one verified legal route. Names, IDs, seeds and prose are excluded from structural signatures. A joint goal's atomic order does not add novelty.

The generator evaluated an explicit curated grid of 278 workflow-conditioned candidates: 278 accepted, 0 rejected. This is not a census of arbitrary random atomic combinations. Failed initial constraints, incompatible terminal equality goals, unsolved bounded searches, bad frames, invalid policy proofs and mismatching replay evidence are rejected by executable gates and dedicated tests. There is no quota backfill with weakened rules. Per-structure ceilings are frozen before sampling; recovery branches share quotas.

Structural depth distribution: 112 tasks at depth 0, 120 at depth 1, 30 at depth 2, eight at depth 3 and eight at depth 4. **46 tasks contain a genuine chain of at least three transitions**. Depth 0 also includes independent multi-tenant actions, so sequence length and difficulty are reported separately. There are 46 tasks with branching in omission counterfactuals; these graphs include mediated edges, not necessarily direct branching prerequisites.

Verified dependencies include:

- Subscription seats -> membership activation, and membership deactivation -> seat reduction.
- Seat reduction -> plan downgrade, and plan upgrade -> seat expansion.
- Invitation -> activation; suspension -> removal.
- Invoice creation -> payment -> correct-period renewal.
- Payment -> reactivation -> capacity change -> membership activation.
- Payment -> reactivation -> plan upgrade -> seat expansion -> membership activation (five transitions, dependency depth four).

The data includes 18 observed dependency edge types and 27 actual `(operation, first rejection code or ALLOWED)` guard interactions. This is not a claim that every masked AST guard was exercised. All 324 retained witnesses are independently Engine/Oracle/replay verified. Necessity claims are limited to completed exclusion searches inside each frozen finite domain and bounds. Search stopping at a solution limit does not establish global completeness.

Service identities: 222 Owner instances and 56 Billing Admin instances. Roles are organization-specific; 28 tasks exercise legitimate multi-tenant operations or tenant-scope refusal. Goal-predicate entity combinations and full operation-domain combinations are separately reported in `coverage.json` and `coverage-details.json`.

Validation is explicitly in-distribution. Test Demo holds out restoration-plus-onboarding, offboarding-plus-downgrade, upgrade-plus-onboarding, restoration-plus-upgrade-plus-onboarding, and offboarding-plus-refund. Their atomic goals and operations are present in Train. Public Demo is not a private blind test. Private-test specification and local ignored-path generation are delivered; no private test instances or secret seeds have been published.

Customer Discovery freezes six actual world/role profiles and three trials each, with 6/8/24/48 Customer turns/Customer calls/Service calls/tools. Victim Service settings remain fixed. Customer model and method strategy are method-controlled and excluded from dataset identity; method profile hashes still record them. Different strategies can be compared under identical worlds, victim, access, tools and budgets. Only independently confirmed successful business violations count; blocked attempts, authorization-only anomalies, repeats and unscored states are separate.

Offline reference-proof competence checks cover every Service instance and achieve 100% independently verified task success. The privileged one-step baseline achieves 23.53% overall task success in Train and Validation, and 0% in the held-out Test Demo. It has evaluator state/domain access but cannot plan prerequisite chains; these are software/baseline checks, not LLM performance.

`pilot-report.json` records actual model observations, replayed scores, model failures and representative replies separately. A stratified pilot is not an official full-release capability score. Any scoring-only correction and exact recorded-request re-evaluation are disclosed there; no model error is removed from its preregistered denominator.

The review package contains 55 stratified examples spanning every family, split, observed depth and actor role. Initial facts, goals, scope, certificates, alternative routes, frames and score explanation are provided in JSON and readable Markdown. **External human review remains pending**; no human ratings were fabricated.

Quantitative engineering targets are met: 278 Service instances, 21 business families, multiple cross-subsystem interaction families, long causal chains, refusal/recovery/alternative-route tasks and a separate Discovery suite. Limits are explicit: one fixed actor, administrator provisioning, public registered names/IDs, 30-day cycles, simulated payments, no tax/proration/credit wallet or real email acceptance. Full-suite real-model comparison and external realism review have not been established by this release.

Actual stratified observations: Service legal completion **5/5**, operational policy handling **1/1** (overall **6/6**). Discovery success **1/3**, with **one unique verified finding**, **one Customer output error**, **one authorization-only anomaly** and **one unscored subsequent operation**. These nine observations were collected through the real gateway; exact response-journal re-evaluation followed a scoring-only correction. Model inputs, full executions and errors were unchanged and verified. The finding activated an inactive member without a free seat; Reference rejects it and the independent Oracle detects both decision and capacity violations in Sandbox. Human interpretation of response rhetoric remains unjudged.
