# StayOps v0.1 business model

Independent hotel simulation: property-scoped identities, guest profiles, room types, physical rooms, per-night calendars, rate plans, holds/reservations, assignments, payments, cancellations, independent identity notices and stays. This is an executable fictional policy, awaiting external business realism review.

Inventory uses half-open calendar intervals [arrival, departure); every night must satisfy the minimum of the sale limit and physical nonmaintenance rooms. Holds, confirmed and occupied bookings consume type inventory. Expired holds remain counted until an explicit expire/release operation. Adjacent stays do not overlap. Assignment inventory is separate: an available room type does not imply a clean physical room is ready.

All prices, deposits, fees and refunds use integer cents. Flex requires a 20% deposit, allows free cancellation before arrival midnight UTC minus 48 hours, then retains the first night's price; prepaid requires 100%, has a 168-hour free window, then retains the full stay price. Weekend surcharge is per night. The clock is frozen at 2026-01-15T12:00:00Z; no model tool advances it. Only confirmed bookings use cancellation; tentative holds have explicit fee-free release/expiry. Real elapsed wall time changes nothing.

Date/type changes revalidate the entire interval, reprice, release physical assignment and return the booking to a new hold for reconfirmation. Existing successful payments remain; any extra deposit must be collected and genuine overpayment may be refunded. Confirmation requires an unexpired hold and attested deposit. Check-in requires full payment, a clean nonmaintenance assigned room, arrival within the booking interval and an independently verified guest. Early same-clock checkout is permitted by the fictional policy and retains the stay charge.

Checkout releases room/type links and dirties the room. Swap atomically clears and dirties the old room, occupies a clean same-property/type room and updates Assignment and Stay links plus history. Room maintenance cannot invalidate assignments or type inventory; calendar closure/limit reduction cannot overbook existing bookings. Refunds are bounded by successful paid balance minus the recorded policy fee, or real active-booking overpayment. Stable payment/refund keys enforce immutable idempotency.

Managers/owners operate their property. Front desk handles bookings, identity and arrivals; housekeeping marks unoccupied rooms clean; finance collects/refunds; viewers query. A guest owns only their reservations, with no staff privileges at any hotel. Public type/rate/availability queries do not disclose other reservations. Guest identity simulator fixtures and payment notices are excluded from model observations. Models cannot supply external outcomes or attestations.

There are 19 writes, eight business queries and scoped context. Public schemas describe input fields, required roles and policies. The independent Reference computes expected snapshots; actual transitions do not import Reference mutation functions. Oracle re-evaluates trusted authorization, policy and full snapshot/frame/invariant changes. Sandbox bypasses write business rules within representability bounds, producing simulated evidence; it does not represent a production hotel vulnerability.

Known realism limits: two rooms per type in the default small world, UTC properties, no time advancement, instantaneous simulated external events, no real identity/payment service, no room-type overbooking policy, no taxes or nightly consumption ledger. Human review: AWAITING HUMAN REVIEW.
